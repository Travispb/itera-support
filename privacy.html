Itera Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

Overview

Itera is designed without developer-operated accounts, advertising, analytics, or media servers. Practice content is stored on your Apple devices and in Apple services connected to your Apple Account.

Storage

Recorded and selected videos remain in your Photos library and may sync through iCloud Photos according to your device settings. Audio recordings, notes, practice history, settings, and recording references are stored in your private CloudKit database. These items use your Apple-managed iCloud storage.

Practice metadata and completed recordings waiting for storage remain in the app's device storage. Itera also keeps a local unfinished-session draft and one previous recovery copy of its metadata. Recovery copies can contain notes, elapsed time, focus tags, session mode, and recording references. They are not intentionally uploaded as active drafts or shared with teachers. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Completed media is retained locally until the destination and its metadata references have been saved successfully. Temporary copies may remain if cleanup cannot finish. Storage use can grow while Photos or iCloud is unavailable; sufficient device and Apple storage are your responsibility.

Teacher Sharing

Itera shares nothing with a teacher unless you use the Apple sharing sheet and choose a recipient. Accepted participants receive read-write access to the shared practice zone, including the practice history, notes, and media available there. This is whole-space access, not a separate permission for each recording. Invite only someone you trust.

For shared video review, Itera places copies in the Apple-hosted shared zone without granting access to your entire Photos library. You can manage or stop access in Settings. Revocation does not delete copies a recipient previously exported, downloaded, or recorded.

Permissions

Microphone access records audio. Camera access records video. Photos access saves and opens practice videos. Itera requests each permission only when the related feature needs it. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Data Collection

The developer does not use practice content for tracking, advertising, profiling, or analytics and does not operate a server that receives it. Apple may process iCloud, Photos, purchase, diagnostic, and App Store information under Apple's own privacy policy.

Personalization uses the context, experience, goals, instrument/voice, and schedule you enter to generate planning suggestions on your device. It does not send recordings to an AI service. CloudKit owner identifiers are used to synchronize and share Apple-hosted data, not for advertising.

Exports and Imports

You may explicitly export practice metadata as JSON, journal entries as CSV, or a recording using the iOS share sheet. These files may contain private information. JSON and CSV exports do not contain audio/video bytes. Recording references can only resolve where the original Photos or CloudKit media is available.

Itera does not encrypt export files or control the destinations and people you select. The recipient's or storage provider's policies apply to those copies. Import reads the file you choose and merges valid data on your device. Import does not grant a new teacher access.

Support and This Website

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to provide and uses it to respond, troubleshoot, and improve Itera. Do not include private recordings or notes unless you deliberately choose to share them for support. GitHub issues are public; your account name and issue contents are visible to others and subject to GitHub's policies.

This static website has no analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or app database. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may receive request information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver and secure the pages. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Support correspondence is retained while addressing your request and for necessary follow-up or legal obligations. You may request deletion of developer-held correspondence through support; the developer cannot remove copies independently retained by Apple, GitHub, or other recipients.

Deletion

Use Settings > Privacy & Support > Erase Itera Data to remove Itera's local data, private CloudKit records, audio assets, and teacher shares. Videos remain in Photos so you remain in control of them and can delete them there.

Individual deleted entries are removed from the active library/history; a previous local recovery snapshot can retain an older version until replaced by a later save. Full erasure clears those recovery copies. Deletion markers containing entry identifiers and timestamps may be retained to prevent removed entries from returning through sync.

If Apple services are unavailable, cloud erasure is retried when Itera can connect again. Existing device backups, files you exported, Photos items, and copies held by other people are outside that erasure. Removing the app deletes its device sandbox but does not itself delete your Photos or CloudKit data.

Your Choices

You can use local practice features without an Itera account, skip personalization, deny recording permissions, manage iCloud through Apple settings, decline teacher sharing, and export or delete your data. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's decisions about recordings, sharing, and public support requests. Do not record others without the appropriate permission.

Changes and Questions

This policy may be updated when Itera's behavior changes. Questions can be submitted through the support page without attaching private recordings or notes.
